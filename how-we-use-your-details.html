How we use your details

We use the email address and search details you send us to put together your sample or list, and to reply to you.

This site doesn't use cookies, ad pixels, or trackers. Some links include a short ?r= tag so that, if you write to us from this site, the email subject can show which page you came from. That tag stays in your browser; it is not stored as a cookie.

If you'd rather not hear from us again, reply and say so.
