How it works

One search, watched and checked by a person.

This is a monitored, hand-checked feed for a search you already care about — not an unfiltered alert dump, and not a replacement for doing your own due diligence.

  1. 01

    You define one search

    Share the profile that matters: NAICS codes, agencies, set-asides, keywords, or a mix. We use that one profile — nothing broader unless you ask.

  2. 02

    We watch SAM.gov for matches

    We look for notices that fit your search and pull the ones that do, with a link back to the original posting on SAM.gov.

  3. 03

    A person checks each notice

    Each item is checked against the source for identity, relevance, the source link, stated eligibility fields, deadline and timezone, and duplicates. Partial or missing data is labeled. This is not eligibility or qualification advice.
