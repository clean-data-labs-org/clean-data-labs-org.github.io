How it works

You tell us one search. We do the reading.

Fewer notices, and only ones that are worth opening. You still read the solicitation and make the call.

  1. 01

    You send one search

    NAICS codes, set-asides, agencies, keywords, location, or whatever mix you already use on SAM.gov. We stick to that search. Nothing broader unless you ask.

  2. 02

    We pull the matches

    We look through new SAM.gov notices for ones that match your search, and keep the link to each original posting.

  3. 03

    A person checks every one

    Does it really fit? Is it still open, and is the deadline right, time zone included? Does the set-aside match SAM.gov? Does the link work? Is it a repeat? Anything that fails gets cut. Anything missing or unclear is marked as such.

Questions

Common questions

What should I send you?

Whatever you already use to search SAM.gov: NAICS codes, any set-asides you want to watch, agencies, keywords, and where you work. The request email has a short template. If something’s missing, we’ll ask before we start.

Will you tell me if I qualify, or whether to bid?

No. We check what the notice says, like the set-aside, NAICS code, and deadline, against SAM.gov. Whether you qualify and whether to bid are your calls, and you should still read the full solicitation.

What happens after the free sample?

We ask for a few lines of feedback on what was useful and what we missed. If you want to keep going, the same search is $99 a month. If not, that’s fine. We never take a card for the sample, and nothing turns into a paid plan on its own.

Can I watch more than one search?

Yes. Each additional search is $49–79 a month, depending on how broad it is. The free sample covers one search.

Are you part of SAM.gov or the government?

No. Clean Data Labs is independent and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by SAM.gov, GSA, or the U.S. government. Every notice we send links back to its official SAM.gov page.

What do you do with my search details?

We use them to put together your sample and reply to you. This page doesn’t use cookies, ad pixels, or trackers. There’s a short page on how we use your details.
